About Us

Sowing wisdom for the harvest

Farmtastic Fun Zone is on a mission to connect kids and families with the story of agriculture — one fair, one booth, one big smile at a time.

Bringing the farm to Texas fairs

Based in Montgomery, Texas, Farmtastic Fun Zone is a fully traveling agricultural education experience built for fairs and rodeos across the state. We roll in with everything needed to create a lively, hands-on farm zone — friendly animals, planting activities, learning videos, crops, crafts, and more — and we set it all up for you.

Every station is designed to spark curiosity in young minds while giving families a memorable day together. From the smallest weekend fair to the biggest multi-week event, we tailor the experience to fit your crowd and your budget.

Our Mission

Educating young minds, growing respect for farming

We educate young minds through hands-on activities, kid-friendly learning videos, and interactive play — inspiring the next generation of thinkers, growing respect for farming, and helping families understand agriculture's importance in our everyday lives.
