Our sister company, Texan Petting Zoo, provides live animal experiences, from gentle goats and lambs to a camel, an alpaca, a kangaroo, and even hatching chicks. These photos show their animals. Farmtastic Fun Zone's own attractions use hands-on props and activities. Ask us when you book if you'd like to add Texan Petting Zoo to your fair.
