Our Attractions

A dozen educational farm experiences in one

Mix and match our hands-on attractions to build the perfect Fun Zone for your fair or rodeo. Every station is interactive, educational, and staffed by our friendly team — and each comes with its own learning video.

🚜

Tractor Track and Trots!

Fun Zone Activity CenterAges ~3–8 (within weight & height limits)

Test your driving skills as you pedal away on authentic tractor replicas or gallop away on an old-fashioned stick horse. This Fun Zone is a great way for children to get their wiggles out while pedaling around an exciting track bordered by a hay fence line for the ultimate FarmTastic experience! *Additional space required.

🐴

Saddle Up Station!

Interactive Learning CenterAges 5 & up

Learn to saddle a pony with hands-on educational fun! In this FarmTastic activity, children and families use real equipment on a life-like pony while watching a kid-friendly, step-by-step tutorial. Come earn your spurs with us!

▶ Watch the video
🌽

Kernel Corn-struction Zone!

Interactive Fun ZoneAges 3 & up

Come join the fun in this sensory play center — a farmer's twist on the traditional sandbox. Children play with dump trucks, backhoes, excavators, and more in a corn-filled box, learning the uses and names of the different equipment through a fun-filled video.

▶ Watch the video
📸

Boots, Scoots, & Photo Shoots!

Fun Zone Memories / Souvenir StationAll Ages

Come make memories with us in our Western Photo Booth, where children and families can dress up as cowboys, farmers, and ranchers. Families choose between various outfits and props to create lasting, fun FarmTastic memories.

▶ Watch the video
🏡

FarmTastic Corral!

Fun Zone Activity CenterAges ~3–8 (within weight & height limits)

Be a rancher using your imagination in this hands-on play center, filled with everything kids need to play like a rancher. Toy corrals, barns, stalls, trucks, stock trailers, tractors, and livestock of all varieties create FarmTastic memories through creative playtime for even the youngest, growing minds!

▶ Watch the video
🎵

Joyful Noise Junction!

Fun Zone Activity CenterAges 5 & up

In this exciting sensory center, children build their creativity and imagination. Stocked with entertaining pieces from the farm, kids get an opportunity to listen and create their own music and sounds in old-fashioned fun!

▶ Watch the video
🌱

Sprout & Grow Garden!

Interactive Learning / Souvenir StationAll Ages

Come grow with us! Children and families learn to become a real farmer. With choices ranging from flowers and melons to fruits and veggies, families plant seeds they can take home and watch grow. A friendly video with step-by-step instructions and at-home care sends them equipped with the knowledge to become a real farmer at home!

▶ Watch the video
🤠

Ropin' Roundup!

Interactive Learning CenterAges 5 & up

Get in on the action with our exciting cowboy/cowgirl roping booth! Children get an opportunity to learn how to rope a steer. With real lassos, a life-like steer, and a kid-friendly educational tutorial and history lesson, children learn how to grab a bull by the horns!

▶ Watch the video
🌻

Scarecrow Creation Station!

Interactive Fun ZoneAll Ages

Learn how to protect the crops the old-fashioned way! Children showcase their creative abilities by building a scarecrow that keeps the pests away. Experience the importance, history, and usefulness of scarecrows in a garden through a kid-friendly learning video while they play.

▶ Watch the video
🧹

Pooper Scooper Trooper!

Fun Zone Activity CenterAll Ages

Life on a farm can get messy! In this activity, children play with the tools and props that mimic daily activities on a farm. Keeping things clean for the animals is a fun way to learn one of the many responsibilities of farm life — these little Farm-Hands will have fun becoming a FarmTastic Pooper Scooper Trooper!

Craft Creations Corner!

Interactive Learning / Souvenir StationAll Ages

Join us in this fun creation station, where you'll sit with one of our friendly staff members making arts and crafts — and lasting memories! Don't forget to show off your artwork to all your friends and family!
